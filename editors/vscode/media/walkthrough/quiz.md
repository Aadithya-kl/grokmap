# Take your first quiz

Choose **Quick** (3 questions) or **Thorough** (4 to 6), then answer in your own words.
Two or three sentences is plenty. Stuck? **I don't know** moves on, and **Finish now** grades what you've answered.

Not a fan of quizzes? Pick **Explain it instead** and describe the code in a paragraph.

Questions are written and graded by the AI model you already have in VS Code, such as GitHub Copilot.
The lines being quizzed and your answers go to that model.

No model in VS Code? Grok-map hands the quiz to **Claude Code**, **Copilot Chat**, **Kilo Code**, **Cline**, **Roo Code** or **Cursor**.
