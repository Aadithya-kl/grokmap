# Share it with your team

A passing quiz is saved in `.grokmap/ledger.jsonl`, a plain file in your repository.

Press **Commit to git** on the result screen. Grok-map commits only the ledger, never your other changes, and never pushes.
Push it with your next change, and everyone's dashboard and gutter will show that you understand that code.

Understanding fades: after 180 days the code turns yellow until someone takes a fresh quiz.
